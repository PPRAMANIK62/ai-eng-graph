## Summary

A short post on where the term "context engineering" came from (Tobi Lütke, then Andrej Karpathy, June 2025) and why it may stick where "prompt engineering" didn't: people guess a term's meaning from its name, and this name guesses right.

## Key claims

- Tobi Lütke is Shopify's CEO. Lütke's definition: "the art of providing all the context for the task to be plausibly solvable by the LLM." (quoted Lütke tweet; the page calls him "Shopify CEO", re-checked 2026-09-27)
- Karpathy's definition: "the delicate art and science of filling the context window with just the right information for the next step." (quoted Karpathy tweet)
- Why the old term failed: "most people's inferred definition is that it's a laughably pretentious term for typing things into a chatbot!" (body)
- Why the new one may stick: "It turns out that inferred definitions are the ones that stick." (body)
