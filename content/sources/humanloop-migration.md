## Summary

Humanloop, a hosted platform for prompt management and evals, was acquired and shut down. This notice told customers that every prompt, version, log and evaluation would be deleted on 2025-09-08, and to export their data and move to another tool, naming Langfuse and Braintrust.

## Key claims

- Deletion date: "Your Humanloop account and all associated data will be permanently deleted on September 8th, 2025." (top)
- Everything goes: "all Files, Versions, Logs, Evaluations, and account settings" deleted and then permanently inaccessible. (same)
- Why: "Following our acquisition, the Humanloop platform will be sunset". The acquirer isn't named. (same)
- Suggested replacements: Langfuse and Braintrust. (Migration options)
- An export tool downloads Files, Versions, Logs, Evaluations and Datapoints as JSON/JSONL. (Export)
