## Summary

OpenAI's short model selection page. As of 2026-09 it ranks nine model and reasoning-effort pairs across its GPT-6 tiers (Luna, Sol, Astra) from cheapest to most capable, and tells you to experiment on the same inputs and keep the lightest setting that is good enough. Read via the page's markdown version (`.md` suffix). Undated.

## Key claims

- Default to the strongest if cost doesn't matter. "If cost and latency aren't a concern, you can default to Astra." (How to think about models and reasoning effort)
- The guidance is only a starting point. "The best way to find the right model for your workflow is to experiment with different models and reasoning settings to see what works." (Experiment)
- Questions to ask: how often the workflow runs ("A frequent automation makes usage and cost add up faster than an occasional project"), how fast you need it, how the output is used, and how much quality matters. (Experiment)
- The rule. "If you can, experiment using the same inputs to compare results and keep the lightest setting that meets your quality bar." (Experiment)
