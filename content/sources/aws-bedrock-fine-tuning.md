## Summary

Bedrock's fine-tuning page, with a table of the models you can fine-tune. The only Anthropic model on it is Claude 3 Haiku, in one region. The rest are Amazon Nova and Titan models and Meta Llama 3.1 to 3.3.

## Key claims

- What fine-tuning means here. "you can train a foundation model to improve performance on specific tasks (known as fine-tuning)." (intro)
- The only Claude model listed: "Anthropic | Claude 3 Haiku | anthropic.claude-3-haiku-20240307-v1:0:200k | us-west-2" (Supported models and Regions for fine-tuning)
- Also listed: Amazon Nova 2 Lite, Nova Lite, Micro, Pro, Canvas, Titan image and embedding models, and Meta Llama 3.1 8B/70B, 3.2 1B/3B/11B/90B, 3.3 70B Instruct. (same table)
