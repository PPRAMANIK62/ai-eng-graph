## Summary

A short article after three big open-weight launches in late April 2026 (Kimi K2.6, MiMo V2.5 Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro). On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index the best open models were 3 to 6 points behind the best closed ones, down from about 13 points a year earlier, at half to one-sixth of the price. The gap stays wide on the hardest reasoning and agentic coding tests, and on hallucination. No author named.

## Key claims

- The gap in index points. "the gap to GPT-5.5, the leading proprietary model, sitting at 6 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index" (top)
- The scores (as of 2026-04-30): Kimi K2.6 and MiMo V2.5 Pro at 54, DeepSeek V4 Pro at 52; GPT-5.5 (xhigh) at 60, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview and Claude Opus 4.7 at 57. (top)
- A year earlier. "just one year ago the highest-scoring open weights model was DeepSeek V3 0324 which achieved 22 on the Intelligence Index, and was ~13 points below the highest-scoring proprietary model, Claude 3.7 Sonnet (Reasoning) at 35." (top)
- Hardest tests. "The gap to proprietary remains wide on the hardest reasoning and agentic coding evaluations." On TerminalBench Hard the open models score 43–46% vs 61% for GPT-5.5 (xhigh); on HLE 34–36% vs 44–45%. (Key takeaways)
- Hallucination. "Omniscience (knowledge + hallucination) shows a large gap to proprietary models". (Key takeaways)
- Size. "The top three most intelligent open weights models are trillion-plus-parameter MoE architectures with permissive licenses." Kimi K2.6 "has 1T total / 32B active parameters", MiMo V2.5 Pro 1T total, DeepSeek V4 Pro 1.6T total. (Key takeaways)
- Where they come from. "The top 10 open weights models on the Intelligence Index are all from China-based AI labs." (Key takeaways)
- Price. "These three models offer comparable intelligence to leading proprietary models at between half to one-sixth of the price." 9 of the 13 models on the intelligence-vs-price Pareto frontier are open weights. (Key takeaways)
